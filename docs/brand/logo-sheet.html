Ledger Tracker logo kit

Built from the four vector files supplied by the owner. The drawing is theirs, untouched. The logo is always the transparent mark, never on a tile or box. The only thing that changes is the ink on the page edges and base line: black on light surfaces (from the owner's lt04), white on dark ones (from lt02).

Which file, where

In the app, <Logo /> does this on its own: black ink on Pearl, white ink on Vault. The files below are for everything else.

What not to do

The wrong ink, or a ground that swallows the colours.

Small sizes

The page edges are about 4% of the mark's width, so below 24 px they soften. That is normal for this drawing; do not thicken it.

Favicon and /logo.svg

Both are transparent and follow the browser's (or viewer's) light or dark scheme, so the ink is always the right one. favicon.ico is a black-ink fallback; apple-touch-icon.png uses white ink because iOS puts a black ground behind transparency.

Measured contrast

WCAG ratio of each colour against the surface it sits on. Logos are exempt, but 3:1 is a fair bar for "visible".

Usage rules (proposed defaults, not supplied by the owner)

  • Do not redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the mark. The only variable is the ink.
  • Do not put the mark on a tile or box, in a gradient, or in a second colour.
  • Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's height on every side.

Still missing: the wordmark

The supplied files contain "LEDGER TRACKER" as live text in the font Satoshi Black, with no font embedded and no outlines, so no lockup is shipped yet.

Without Satoshi the letters fall back to another font and the spacing breaks. In all four supplied files the words are white, so on the two light versions the wordmark is invisible. Ask the designer for the same files with the text converted to outlines, and a black wordmark on the light versions.
